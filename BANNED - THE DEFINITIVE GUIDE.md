# BANNED: THE DEFINITIVE GUIDE [COMPRESSED]

Core: Every banned element = form substitute content, decoration substitute precision, familiar rhythm substitute earned meaning. Fix: be specific, be embodied, show consequence. Accumulation principle — one bad element fine, density of same patterns across characters = problem.

---

## PART 1: CONSTRUCTIONS

### 1.1 Sequential Action Pairs

Banned: ", then" between actions. Replace with fluid motion revealing psychology.

### 1.2 Vague Interiority Placeholder

Banned: "something [verb] in/behind [body part]". Name exactly what happen.

### 1.3 Anthropomorphized Silence

Banned: "silence [verb]" (stretches/sits/hangs/grows). Show who break it, who endure, what it cost.

### 1.4 Negation Formula

Banned: "not X, but Y". Commit to what it actually is.

### 1.5 Triple-Beat Lists

Banned: three nouns/fragments sequence for emphasis. Cut or integrate.

### 1.6 Staccato Verb Fragments

Banned: "[Verb]. [Verb]. [Verb]." Vary syntax.

### 1.7 Conjunction Starts (Overuse)

Banned: sentence-start "And"/"But" as performative rhythm. Use sparingly for earned emphasis only.

### 1.8 Standalone "Because" Fragments

Banned: "Because [X]" as own sentence. Show reason through action.

### 1.9 Hedged Reactions

Banned: "A [reaction] that isn't quite [itself]". Describe gesture directly.

### 1.10 Echo-Line Poetics

Banned: two consecutive parallel-structure sentences where second just rephrases first. Must develop or cut.

### 1.11 Mood-Prop Negation

Banned: "[action] + [negation]" (grabs soy sauce doesn't need, smokes cigarette doesn't want). Show why character act or cut.

### 1.12 Meta-Narrative Intrusion

Banned: narration referencing story mechanics ("scene's not over", "this was real moment"). Stay in character POV.

### 1.13 Atmospheric Front-Loading

Banned: scene open with weather/skyline/architecture before character. Start with character.

### 1.14 Gravitational Metaphors

Banned: physics language for connection (pulled/drawn/orbits/gravity/magnetic). Attraction as choice.

### 1.15 Suspension Phrases

Banned: "hangs in the air / hangs between them". Show aftermath, not suspended atmosphere.

### 1.16 Impact Similes

Banned: "like a blow/punch/freight train". Show actual physical sensation.

### 1.17 Ripple/Stillness Similes

Banned: "like a stone in still water", "rippled through", "stillness shattered". Show impact directly.

### 1.18 Precision/Control Cluster

Banned: surgical precision, practiced ease, economical movement, calculated, calibrated, methodical, clinical, exacting. Show actual movement.

### 1.19 Quality/Texture Defaults

Banned: velvet, silk over steel, steel (spine/voice/gaze), iron (grip/will), granite, marble. Find different texture.

### 1.20 Blank Desire Statements

Banned: "wanted her" / "needed him" / "ache for her" without specific object/why. Specify what wanted + why.

### 1.21 Articulation by Proxy

Banned: "a look that said everything", "didn't need words", "she knew what he meant". Show actual exchange.

### 1.22 Hollow Restraint

Banned: "held it together", "swallowed it down", "kept it inside", "bottled it up". Name what exactly being held + what leak when it fail.

### 1.23 Aestheticized Damage

Banned: stylized dysfunction as character shorthand (another drink didn't need, no sleep in days, coffee + cigarettes + spite). Show cost, not decorate.

### 1.24 Faux-Intellectual Aphorism

Banned: lines sound profound but zero narrative content ("we are all stories in end", "maybe love was real war"). Must be real thought this specific character have in this moment.

### 1.25 Misapplied Epic Tone

Banned: "changed everything", "broke forever", "world held its breath". Scale to proportion.

### 1.26 Elegant Variation

Banned: descriptor cycling instead of names ("the older man" / "the younger woman"). Use names unless descriptor do specific work (POV, power dynamic, emotional distance).

### 1.27 False Range Construction

Banned: "from X to Y" where X + Y not on same measurable scale. Cut if no real scale exist.

### 1.28 Superficial Analysis as Narration

Banned: present participle phrase attached to sentence for editorialize meaning (highlighting, underscoring, reflecting, emphasizing, showcasing, symbolizing). Action must speak for itself.

### 1.29 Negative Parallelism

Banned: "not only... but", "it's not about X it's about Y". State directly.

### 1.30 Simile-as-Adverb

Banned: "with the [noun] of someone [verb]ing". Just describe behavior directly.

### 1.31 Trailing Participle Pile-Up

Banned: multiple consecutive sentences ending with ",[verb]ing". One fine. Two per paragraph max. Vary structure.

## PART 3: GUIDELINES

### 3.1 Root Problem

All banned elements fail same way: **simulate instead create**, **interchangeable across characters**, **tell instead show**, **default to familiar**. Accumulation principle — one element fine, density kill.

### 3.2 Corrective Framework

**Physical tells:** How THIS character show emotion? Specific outlet.
**Vague interiority:** What actually happen? What observer see?
**Metaphors:** Clarify or decorate? If decorate, cut.
**Dialogue tags:** Can line carry own tone? If adverb needed, rewrite.
**Transitions:** Who break first? What happen during pause?
**Endings:** What action/decision/consequence close beat?

### 3.3 What To Do Instead

- Identify each character specific stress responses. Displacement activities show coping.
- Name specific emotion: anger, fear, grief, shame, desire, relief.
- Show thought pattern + behavioral consequence.
- Describe sensation directly. Ground comparison in character frame.
- Start with character in action/decision. Environment through their perception.
- End on micro-action, dialogue shift, consequence. Cut earlier if possible.
- Second line must add info or cut.

### 3.4 Pre-Draft Protocol

- [ ] Vary sentence rhythm
- [ ] No triple-beat / staccato fragments
- [ ] Start with character presence, not atmosphere
- [ ] End on action/decision/consequence
- [ ] No adverb dialogue tags
- [ ] No "something" as emotion placeholder
- [ ] No silence/atmosphere as active force
- [ ] Name specific emotions
- [ ] Each character distinct physical tells
- [ ] No jaw/throat/hand clichés
- [ ] Breath only if change tempo or power
- [ ] No gravitational/temperature metaphors
- [ ] No dead similes
- [ ] Every comparison clarify, not decorate

### 3.5 Mid-Draft Flagging

Writing → Stop + Ask:

- ", then" → psychology or choreography?
- "something" → what exactly?
- adverb after "said" → can line carry tone?
- voice drops/tightens → show through rhythm/action?
- jaw/throat/hand → how THIS character show restraint?
- "finally" → who yield?
- domestic activity → create friction or pad?
- "changed everything" → moment actually that big?

### 3.6 The Final Test

1. **Specificity:** Could any line appear unchanged in different story?
2. **Interchangeability:** Could this tell/metaphor/ending belong to any character?
3. **Consequence:** Does something change because of this scene/beat/line?
4. **Decoration:** Does this metaphor clarify or ornament?
5. **Earned:** Has emotional beat been built to or just announced?

### 3.7 When Rules Conflict

- **Specificity > brevity.** Longer line doing work beat shorter line decorating.
- **Character voice > ban list.** Character would genuinely use banned term in dialogue? Flag, justify, use sparingly. Narration no excuse.
- **Clarity > rhythm.** Clunky sentence communicating beat smooth sentence obscuring.
- **Precision > convention.** Unfamiliar construction fitting beat familiar one not.

### 3.8 AI Pattern

Banned patterns = identifiable AI tells. Underlying failure same: regression to statistically common instead of specific. Corrective: be specific, ground in character perception, make gesture do work, let consequence emerge from action.

### 3.9 Elegant Variation

Use names unless descriptor do work: establishing POV, marking emotional distance, shifting power dynamic. If descriptor just avoid name, use name.

### 3.10 Narrator Distance

Narration stay inside character perception or scene physical reality. If action need interpretation: (1) action underwritten, beef up (2) interpretation belong in character interiority (3) cut, trust reader.
